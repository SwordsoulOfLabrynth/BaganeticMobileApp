Delete your Baganetic account

You can permanently delete your account from Profile → Delete account in the Baganetic app. You will be asked to confirm your password.

If you cannot access the app or have already uninstalled it, email us from the account email address and include the subject “Delete my Baganetic account”.

Request account deletion by email

What is deleted

We delete the account, profile, stored trips, favourites, profile image, AI history, and community activity associated with that account, unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, or law. We aim to process external requests promptly after verifying that the request comes from the account owner.
