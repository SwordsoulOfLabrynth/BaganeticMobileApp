Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Baganetic is a Bagan travel guide. This policy explains what the app stores, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Data stored on your device

Pagoda content, map tiles you view, trip plans, favourites, and a local copy of community posts may be stored in SQLite and the map cache so the app can work offline.

Account and profile data

If you create an account, Firebase Authentication processes your email address and password credential. Baganetic may store your display name, profile photo, trips, favourites, AI chat history, and community activity in Firebase services for your account.

Community content

Tips, questions, comments, and votes you publish can be visible to other signed-in travellers. Do not post personal information you do not want others to see.

AI assistant

Signed-in AI questions are sent to Baganetic’s Cloud Function and then to its hosted AI provider, with limited pagoda, trip, and community context. Prompts are shortened and usage is rate-limited. AI responses can be wrong and should not be treated as official travel advice.

Diagnostics

Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports and technical diagnostics so we can find and fix app reliability problems.

Tester feedback

If you submit feedback through the Baganetic tester form, we store the answers you provide, optional name or contact details, and device information only to improve the app and follow up on a reported issue. Tester feedback is visible only to Baganetic administrators.

Location and maps

If you allow it, the map uses your device location only while the map screen is open to show your position. Baganetic does not upload your GPS location to its servers. Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap Humanitarian tiles, with OpenStreetMap.org as a fallback.

Your choices and account deletion

You can use guest mode without creating an account. Signed-in users can delete their account in the Profile section of the app. You can also request deletion after uninstalling the app using our account-deletion page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact admin@baganetic.com.
